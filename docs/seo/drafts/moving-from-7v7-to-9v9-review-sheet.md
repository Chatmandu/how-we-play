## Facts checked against official sources

| Item | England 7v7 | England 9v9 | FA reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026/27 age groups | U10–U11 | U12–U13 | [Game formats](https://learn.englandfootball.com/articles-and-resources/coaching/resources/2022/what-is-a-format-in-football) |

| Offside | No | Yes | Same guidance |
| Retreat line | Yes | No | Same guidance |
| Sideline restarts | U10: pass or dribble in; U11: throw-in | Throw-ins | Same guidance |
| Powerplay | Yes | No | Same guidance |
| Heading in matches | Not allowed | Permitted | Same guidance |
| Rolling substitutions | Yes | Yes | Same guidance |

The FA's [heading guidance](https://www.englandfootball.com/participate/learn/brain-health/heading-in-football) says heading is a low priority in U12/U13 training and sets tight limits for any introduction.

**Maths:** 55 × 37 = 2,035 m²; 73 × 46 = 3,358 m². The percentage increase is `(3358 / 2035 - 1) × 100 = 65.0%` (rounded). These are **recommended** pitch areas, not measurements of every ground.

**Competition caveat:** [Liverpool County FA's FutureFit policy](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/cfa/liverpoolfa/files/about/strategy-and-policies/lcfa-futurefit-policy.ashx?la=en) addresses how local affiliated leagues handle the transition. The article should advise checking individual competition rules without presenting local arrangements as nationwide exceptions.

**Detail to resolve:** Some general FutureFit wording discusses pass-ins/dribble-ins continuing through 7v7, whereas the more detailed 2026/27 FA 7v7 article specifies **U10 pass/dribble-ins and U11 throw-ins**. This draft follows the detailed age-specific guidance. Please confirm with the target competition's rules before release.
